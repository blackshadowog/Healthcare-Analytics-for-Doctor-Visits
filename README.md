## Dataset summary
5,190 records and 13 columns. The analysis covers data inspection, missing-value checks, gender distribution, age distribution, doctor-visit distribution, average visits by gender, illness score vs. average visits, and age/visits/gender relationships.

## Key findings
- 5,190 records and 13 columns.
- No missing values in the supplied fields.
- 2,702 female and 2,488 male records.
- Average recorded visits: 0.36 for females and 0.24 for males.
- Mean visits increase from 0.08 at illness score 0 to 0.81 at illness score 5.
- 4,141 records have zero recorded visits.
- Maximum recorded visits: 9.
